Zini CRM - Account Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account directly from the Zini CRM application.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Zini CRM app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to Profile.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

What happens after deletion?

Once confirmed, your account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Data that will be deleted

  • Account information
  • Personal profile
  • Associated personal data
